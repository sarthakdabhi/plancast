YOUR MAC DOES THE WORK.

Local AI.
One less service.

Qwen3 writes the dialogue through a Plancast-managed llama.cpp runtime. Pocket TTS gives it two voices. No Ollama or LM Studio needed.

First setup downloads the software and models. After setup, local generation keeps your plan on your Mac. Node is bundled; Python, uv, and FFmpeg are managed for you.

A few things
to know.

Does my plan leave my Mac?

Not with the default local providers. Setup downloads assets, but local generation processes your plan on your Mac. If you explicitly select OpenAI, plan content and dialogue are sent to its APIs after a disclosure. Your API account pays for that usage.

Can I change the voices or the model?

Yes. Jane and George are the defaults; Alba and Marius are also supported. The model catalog includes Qwen3 4B, 8B, and 14B. Smaller models have not been benchmarked here. See the configuration guide for options.

How accurate are the briefings?

Plancast checks structured dialogue and source references before generating speech. These checks cannot guarantee semantic accuracy or complete coverage. This is an early prerelease; use the source document to verify decisions and important details.

Is Plancast free?

Plancast is open source under the MIT license. Local generation has no cloud API charges and uses your own hardware. Downloaded models, runtimes, and voice recordings retain their respective licenses.
